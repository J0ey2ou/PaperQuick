# PaperQuick v3.2 · 使用指南

这一版把“阅读总结”扩展为可以回顾阅读、整理主题、寻找后续文献的独立窗口，同时加入 Word 引用、文档互转和附件并排阅读。[下载 v3.2 Windows 版](https://github.com/J0ey2ou/PaperQuick/releases/download/v3.2.0/PaperQuick-v3.2-Windows.zip)。升级前先退出软件并备份完整文献库，安装与升级步骤见 [README](../README.md)。

## 先从需要的功能开始

| 要完成的事 | 入口与说明 |
| --- | --- |
| 把 PDF、Word 收纳到课题 | 文献分类 → 导入 PDF / Word；支持多选与文件夹导入 |
| 关联附件和补充材料网址 | 文献分类右键 → 附件管理 / 比对阅读；或阅读页 → 正文与附件 |
| 正文与附件分别翻页比对 | 选中 PDF / Word 附件 → 并排阅读；[详细操作](documents-and-attachments.md) |
| Word 转 PDF，PDF 转 Word | 阅读页 → Word / PDF 转换；可将结果自动关联到当前文献 |
| 在 Word 中插入引用和参考文献 | 文献分类 → Word 引用；[详细操作](word-citations.md) |
| 回顾阅读、整理关键词和寻找新文献 | 右上方 → 阅读总结；继续阅读下方说明 |

Word 分页预览、Word 转 PDF 及引用插入需要桌面版 Microsoft Word。PDF 转 Word、PDF 阅读和 DOCX 导入归档不依赖 Word。PDF 转 Word 可选“可编辑文字”（提取正文和逐页分隔，不还原图表与复杂排版；扫描无文字页保留为图片）或“保留页面外观”（每页图片，文字不可直接编辑）；两种模式都不进行 OCR。上述整理功能在本机完成，不使用外部 AI；联网检索和 DOI 补全需要网络。

## 打开阅读总结

启动 `文献直达.exe`，点击右上方 **阅读总结**。原页面保持不动；关闭弹窗后继续原来的阅读或查询。四个栏目分别是 **阅读回顾、主题与检索、引用与被引、主题追踪**。

## 回顾昨天，或这一周的阅读

1. 在 **阅读回顾** 选择 **昨天 / 今天 / 近七天**。想查看某一天，填写日期后点击 **查看日期**。
2. 在课题选项中选择全部课题或一个具体课题。
3. 查看读过的文献和时长。点击某篇文献查看摘要、笔记和摘录，点击 **继续阅读** 回到文章。
4. 用 **复制总结** 或 **导出**，把回顾留在自己的工作记录中。

仅在文献打开且阅读页面处于前台时计时。打开总结、切换到其他软件或收起窗口都会暂停。这个时长反映阅读页面的停留时间，不判断是否已经理解文章。旧版本没有记录的时长无法补算；回顾内容使用目前保存的摘要和笔记，因此编辑笔记后，过去日期的回顾也会显示更新后的内容。

## 整理主题关键词，生成下一次检索

1. 打开 **主题与检索**，选择 **课题文献库** 或 **所选时段的阅读**。
2. 查看主题关键词及其相关文献数量。关键词来自已有标题、摘要和文献关键词，无需把全文交给外部 AI。
3. 双击感兴趣的词加入下方输入框，也可以直接编辑；多个词用分号分隔。
4. 选择 **PubMed / Web of Science / Google Scholar / Europe PMC**，点击 **生成检索式**。检索式仍可修改。
5. 点击 **复制检索式** 自行使用，或 **打开检索网站**。选择 Web of Science 时，检索式会先复制，请在打开的高级检索页面粘贴。

这些词和检索式用于帮你起步，可以根据研究对象、疾病、方法等继续增删，不等同于已经完成一套系统综述检索方案。

## 沿引用关系寻找未收藏的文献

1. 打开 **引用与被引**，选择课题并查找一篇已收藏的文献，作为探索起点。
2. 选择 **它引用的文献**、**引用它的文献** 或两个方向一起查看，点击 **查找关联文献**。
3. 结果会略过已收藏文献，并标明与起点的引用关系。选中条目后，点击 **查找原文**，或选择目标课题后 **收藏文献**。

这个功能查询 Europe PMC 已收录的公开引用关系，无需密钥，主要面向生命科学与医学。它不包含所有商业数据库中的引用；没有返回结果不代表原文没有参考文献或没有被引用。若只查到一个方向，另一方向暂时无法连接，已经找到的结果仍会保留。结果较多时会提示当前只展示其中一部分。

## 关注一个主题，持续查看新文献

1. 在 **主题与检索** 点击 **关注这个主题**，或在 **主题追踪** 点击 **新增主题**。
2. 填写容易识别的主题名称，检查或修改 Europe PMC 检索式，点击 **保存主题**。
3. 点击 **查找新文献** 手动检查。首次检查默认关注近 30 天发表的研究；结果过多时，可缩小检索条件。
4. 如需持续关注，勾选 **每天检查一次（软件运行时）** 并保存。软件运行且可联网时每天检查一次；退出软件后不会运行，也不会唤醒电脑。当日连接失败不会反复自动尝试，你可以随时手动重试。
5. 从结果中选择文献，继续 **查找原文 / 下载归档**，或 **收藏到课题**。

在 **历次发现** 中切换以前的检查结果。**撤回这次发现** 会收起本批列表；点击 **恢复这次发现** 可重新显示。它不会撤销收藏，更不会删除已下载的 PDF、批注或阅读笔记。

主题追踪查看 Europe PMC 当前可检索的新发表文献。文献收录可能有延迟，检索也可能只返回部分结果，不能把它当作完整、无遗漏的研究监测。结果会明确提示是否还有更多文献。

## 数据和已有功能

阅读、笔记、主题整理和检查历史保存在本机。关键词整理不需要联网；主题追踪发送保存的检索式，引用探索发送起点文献 DOI 或标题及相关文献编号，不上传 PDF 正文、笔记或完整文献库。打开检索网站、查原文和下载需要网络；是否能获取全文取决于开放情况和机构权限。

分类、自动命名、PDF / Word 阅读、附件比对、图谱、皮肤与托盘功能均可配合使用。EndNote 仍支持只读导入、附件归档、差异预览和 XML 交换；**已有 EndNote 条目的全自动双向写回尚未实现**。完整安装、备份及 EndNote 使用范围见 [README](../README.md)。

## English

Download [PaperQuick v3.2 for Windows](https://github.com/J0ey2ou/PaperQuick/releases/download/v3.2.0/PaperQuick-v3.2-Windows.zip). This release combines the four-tab recap with Word citations, document conversion and attachment comparison. Exit and back up the complete library before upgrading; see [installation steps](../README.md#english).

For document import, use **文献分类 → 导入 PDF / Word**. Open **正文与附件** in the reader to add files or URLs and compare PDF/Word supplements beside the main document. **Word / PDF 转换** saves a new output and can attach it to the current paper. See [Documents and attachments](documents-and-attachments.md#english). Installed desktop Microsoft Word is required for Word pagination, Word-to-PDF conversion and citation insertion; PDF-to-Word conversion, PDF reading and DOCX import/storage work without it. PDF-to-Word offers editable-text extraction with page separation, or page images that preserve appearance. Text mode does not restore charts or complex layouts, and pages without extractable text remain images. Neither mode performs OCR.

For writing, open **文献分类 → Word 引用** to insert citations and a bibliography with 31 bundled formats or additional CSL styles. See the [Word citation guide](word-citations.md#english-quick-start).

To review reading and discover papers, click **阅读总结** at the top right. The recap opens in its own window and leaves your current page in place.

### Review your reading

Open **阅读回顾**. Choose yesterday, today, the last seven days or a specific date, and filter by project. Select a paper to revisit its abstract, notes and excerpts, continue reading, or copy/export the whole recap.

Time is counted only while the loaded reading page is in front of you. The recap, another app or a hidden window pauses it. This measures time on the page, not comprehension. Previously unrecorded time cannot be recovered. Content reflects currently saved notes and abstracts, including later edits.

### Turn topics into a search

In **主题与检索**, choose a project's library or papers read during the selected period. Keywords are prepared locally from titles, abstracts and supplied keywords. Double-click suggested terms to add them, or edit the terms yourself, separated by semicolons.

Choose **PubMed, Web of Science, Google Scholar or Europe PMC**, generate the query, then edit, copy or open it. For Web of Science, paste the copied query into its advanced search page. These suggestions help you start; refine them for your research question before treating them as a systematic review search strategy.

### Explore references and citing papers

In **引用与被引**, choose a collected paper, select references, citing papers or both, then search. Papers already in your library are omitted. Select a finding to search for its original text or collect it into a project. Its relationship to the starting paper is retained.

This uses **Europe PMC's public citation network without an API key**, mainly covering life sciences and medicine. It is not a complete index of every citation. If one direction cannot be retrieved, successful results from the other direction remain available. Large result sets are marked as partial.

### Follow a research topic

Choose **关注这个主题** from the search tab, or add a theme in **主题追踪**. Name it, review its Europe PMC query and save. Click **查找新文献** to check manually; the first check looks at the past 30 days by default.

Daily checking is optional: enable **每天检查一次（软件运行时）** and save. It runs once per day while PaperQuick is running and connected, does not run after exit, and does not wake your computer. A failed automatic check is not repeated all day; you can retry manually.

Use **历次发现** to revisit earlier checks. Withdrawing a discovery batch hides its list and can be undone. **Collected records, PDFs, annotations and reading notes are never removed by withdrawing a batch.** Indexing delays and partial result sets mean tracking cannot guarantee every new publication is found.

### Privacy and compatibility

Notes, reading records, locally prepared topics and discovery history remain on your computer. Tracking sends the saved query to Europe PMC; citation searches send the starting title/DOI and required publication identifiers, not your PDFs, notes or complete library. Searching and downloading require internet access and remain subject to full-text access rights.

Existing organization, naming, reading, graph, theme and tray features remain available. EndNote support is still read-only import, attachment archiving, change previews and XML exchange; **automatic write-back to existing EndNote records is not implemented**. See the [README](../README.md#english) for installation and backup guidance.
